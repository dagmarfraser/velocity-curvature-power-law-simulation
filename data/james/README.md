# James et al. (2020) — Bumblebee Locomotion

Free-walking trajectories of bumblebees on an arena floor, tracked from overhead video.

**Citation:** James, L., Davies, T. G. E., Lim, K. S., & Reynolds, A. (2020). Do bumblebees have signatures? Demonstrating the existence of a speed-curvature power law in *Bombus terrestris* locomotion patterns. *PLoS ONE*, 15(1), e0226393.
**DOI:** [10.1371/journal.pone.0226393](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0226393)
**Data (supplementary):** [10.1371/journal.pone.0226393.s001](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0226393.s001)

**Used in this project:** exploratory — free locomotion at 4 Hz is outside the range this project's noise-characterisation methods can meaningfully handle, so it's excluded from the main pooled analysis but included as a high-noise stress test of the six analytical pipelines.

## Redistribution

Open access supplementary data via the DOI above; not separately redistributed here.

## About the data

- *Bombus terrestris* (buff-tailed bumblebee), centroid tracking via CTRAX, 4 frames per second.
- Three replicates, each with appetitive/aversive/combined training conditions and matched controls.
