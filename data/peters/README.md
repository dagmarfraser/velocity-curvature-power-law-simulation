# Peters, Wang & Limanowski — Visuomotor Incongruence Ellipse Tracing

Continuous ellipse tracing on a graphics tablet under manipulated visual feedback (spatial offset and temporal delay conditions), plus a clean congruent baseline.

**Citation:** Peters, F., Wang, P., & Limanowski, J. (2025). Action enhances the detection of visuomotor incongruence: A comparison of matched spatial vs temporal manipulations along identical trajectories. *bioRxiv*.
**DOI:** [10.1101/2025.06.18.660374](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.06.18.660374)
**Preregistration:** [osf.io/39tch](https://osf.io/39tch)

**Used in this project:** exploratory only, closed — a planned follow-up recording to rule out a device-driver confound (Windows "Enhance Pointer Precision") did not become available in time, so results from this dataset are not part of the primary analysis.

## Redistribution

Shared with this project directly by the original authors (University of Greifswald) for reanalysis, not for further redistribution. **Individual movement trajectories are considered personalised data by the original authors — do not display or publish individual-participant raw trajectories.** Group-level statistics and aggregate metrics are fine to report. Contact the original authors for access to the raw data itself.

## About the data

- One by Wacom CTL-672 graphics tablet, pen input, ~75 Hz.
- 33 participants (32 valid), continuous ellipse tracing under congruent, spatially-offset, or temporally-delayed visual feedback of their own movement.
