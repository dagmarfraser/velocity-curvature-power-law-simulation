# Cook et al. — Autistic vs Neurotypical Shape Tracing

Autistic and neurotypical adults tracing nine template shapes of varying angular frequency on a digitising tablet.

**Citation:** Cook, J. L., Fraser, D. S., Hickman, L. J., Brewer, R., & Huh, D. (2026). The relationship between speed and curvature differs in autistic and non-autistic tracing movements. *Scientific Reports*.
**DOI:** [10.1038/s41598-026-37067-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-37067-z)
(Preprint: Cook et al., 2023, bioRxiv, [10.1101/2023.03.23.532745](https://doi.org/10.1101/2023.03.23.532745))

**Used in this project:** core empirical validation dataset. This is the dataset behind the clinical divergence threshold (|Δβ| ≈ 0.03) that motivates the whole simulation study.

## Redistribution

Birmingham data, not redistributed through this repository. Will be shared via UBIRA eData in due course.

## About the data

- WACOM Cintiq 22HD pen display, 133 Hz.
- 21 autistic and 19 neurotypical participants, matched for age and IQ.
- Nine template shapes spanning a range of angular frequencies (near-circular through rounded-square), up to 5 trials per shape per participant.
