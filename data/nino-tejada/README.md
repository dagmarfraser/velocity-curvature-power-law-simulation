# Niño-Tejada et al. — MoCap and IMU Upper-Limb Joint Angle Dataset

Synchronised motion capture and wrist/chest IMU recordings for upper-limb joint angle estimation.

**Citation:** Niño, K. (2025). MoCap and IMU Dataset for Upper Limb Joint Angle Estimation [Data set]. Zenodo.
**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.15778950](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15778950)
**License:** CC BY 4.0

**Used in this project:** not currently used. Downloaded as an alternative candidate alongside the ULTRA-MoCap dataset for the Coherence Gap sub-project; ULTRA-MoCap was the one actually adopted. Kept here pending a decision on whether to remove it entirely.

## Redistribution

CC BY 4.0 via Zenodo (link above); not separately redistributed here.

## About the data

- OptiTrack motion capture plus two Mbientlab MetaMotionRL IMUs (wrist, chest), 50 Hz.
- Six recording sessions, arm flexion/shoulder-raise/combined movements.
