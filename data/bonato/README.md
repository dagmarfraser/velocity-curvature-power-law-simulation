# Bonato — Complex Upper-Limb Movements (PhysioNet)

Hand-trajectory recordings from ten healthy adults performing a range of upper-limb movements (writing, drawing shapes, and freeform tasks), used in the original study to identify motor primitives.

**Citation:** Miranda, J. G. V., Daneault, J.-F., Vergara-Diaz, G., Torres, Â. F. S. de O., Quixadá, A. P., Fonseca, M. de L., Vieira, J. P. B. C., dos Santos, V. S., Figueiredo, T. C. da, Pinto, E. B., Peña, N., & Bonato, P. (2018). Complex upper-limb movements are generated by combining motor primitives that scale with the movement size. *Scientific Reports*, 8(1).
**DOI:** [10.1038/s41598-018-29470-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-29470-y)
**Data (PhysioNet):** [physionet.org/content/culm/1.0.0](https://physionet.org/content/culm/1.0.0/)

**Used in this project:** not currently used. Downloaded as a candidate exploratory addition (rich variety of drawn shapes, 3D hand trajectories) but never formally adopted — no importer was written and it isn't part of the pre-registration. Kept here pending a decision on whether to remove it entirely.

## Redistribution

Open Access via PhysioNet (link above); not separately redistributed here.

## About the data

- 3D hand trajectory recordings, Motion Analysis Lab, Spaulding Rehabilitation Hospital.
- Ten healthy adults performing a battery of upper-limb motor tasks, including several drawn-shape conditions (ellipse, circle, spiral, triangle, and others).
