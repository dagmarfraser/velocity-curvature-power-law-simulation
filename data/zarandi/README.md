# Zarandi et al. (2023) — Practised Ellipse Drawing

Continuous ellipse tracing on a digitising tablet, recorded from experienced participants at three practised paces (slow, normal, fast).

**Citation:** Zarandi, Z., Stucchi, N. A., Fadiga, L., & Pozzo, T. (2023). The effect of the preferred hand on drawing movement. *Scientific Reports*, 13, 8264.
**DOI:** [10.1038/s41598-023-34861-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-34861-x)

**Used in this project:** core empirical validation dataset.

## Redistribution

Not redistributed here. The dataset accompanies the paper above; contact the corresponding author or check the paper's data-availability statement for the current access route.

## About the data

- WACOM Bamboo Slate digitising tablet, recorded at 100 Hz (the published paper states 200 Hz, but the raw timestamp column increments in steps consistent with 100 Hz — worth checking directly against the timestamps if you use this data rather than trusting the paper's stated rate).
- Participants traced a template ellipse from memory after the template was removed, practised beforehand with a metronome, at three paces.
- 14 participants available for this project's own reanalysis; 133 trials total.
