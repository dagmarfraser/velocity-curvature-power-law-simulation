# Grouvel et al. (2023) — Gait Dataset (Optical + IMU)

Synchronised optical motion capture and wearable IMU recordings of overground walking, independent hardware from the Santos et al. dataset, used as a cross-check.

**Citation:** Grouvel et al. (2023). *Scientific Data*.
**DOI:** [10.1038/s41597-023-02077-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-02077-3)
**Data (Zenodo):** [10.5281/zenodo.7681870](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7681870)

**Used in this project:** side-project (Coherence Gap sub-project) — a hardware cross-check for the same position/acceleration spectral-agreement question examined in the Santos dataset, not part of the main pre-registered pipeline comparison.

## Redistribution

Open access via Zenodo (link above); not separately redistributed here.

## About the data

- Qualisys optical motion capture at 100 Hz plus GaitUp Physilog 6S IMUs (native 256 Hz), 8 body segments.
- 10 participants, several walking-speed conditions.
