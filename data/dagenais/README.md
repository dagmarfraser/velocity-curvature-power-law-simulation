# Dagenais et al. (2021) — Elephant Trunk Kinematics

3D marker-tracked trunk-tip trajectories from an African elephant performing reaching and manipulation tasks.

**Citation:** Dagenais, P., Hensman, S., Haechler, V., & Milinkovitch, M. C. (2021). Elephants evolved strategies reducing the biomechanical complexity of their trunk. *Current Biology*, 31(21), 4727–4737.
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.cub.2021.08.029](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2021.08.029)
**Data:** [Zenodo, 10.5281/zenodo.17094445](https://zenodo.org/records/17094445)

**Used in this project:** core empirical validation dataset — about as far from human tablet drawing as this project gets while still testing the same power law.

## About the data

- Qualisys motion capture, 10 infrared cameras, 100 Hz, retroreflective markers along the dorsal surface of the trunk.
- One adult male African elephant ("Chishuru"), 48 trials: reach, twist, exploration, relaxed trunk, and prehension of objects of varying size and mass.
- Both raw marker positions and the authors' own fitted ellipse cross-sections of the trunk are included.
