# ULTRA-MoCap — Synchronised Upper-Limb Motion Capture, IMU and sEMG

Synchronised optical motion capture, inertial measurement, and surface EMG recordings of the upper limb across five tasks.

**Citation:** Fritsche, L., Camacho, D., Hossain, A., et al. (2026). ULTRA-MoCap: a synchronized motion capture, IMU and sEMG dataset of the upper limb. *Scientific Data*.
**DOI:** [10.1038/s41597-026-06687-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-06687-5)

**Used in this project:** side-project (Coherence Gap sub-project) — used to characterise how much acceleration-reconstruction error propagates through curvature and the power-law exponent, not part of the main pre-registered pipeline comparison.

## Redistribution

Not redistributed through this repository. Obtain via the paper's own data-availability statement.

## About the data

- Optical motion capture markers plus wrist/elbow/shoulder IMUs and sEMG, synchronised.
- 13 participants, five upper-limb tasks including arm-swing and crossbody-reach movements.
